A school bag that tells you how much it should carry.

Every Tola bag has its own empty weight and the loaded weight advised for the child's class sewn into the strap. Weigh it at the door and you know if it is too heavy.

Bags are too heavy, and the rule is clear

10%Of body weight is the limit in India's School Bag Policy 2020 (NCERT).
21.65%Average load relative to body weight for Class 6 children in Pune and Hyderabad, 1,321 children measured (Indian Pediatrics, 2020).
77.7%Of 2,992 surveyed parents said handling a heavy bag is a problem (NCERT policy survey).

Sources: NCERT policy, Indian Pediatrics. The government has also asked BIS to review its 1982 school-bag standard (Mint).

The Tola Daily

  • Load label. A woven tag on the strap: empty weight in grams, and the advised weight of the whole loaded bag for the class band.
  • Light by design. Target empty weight under 600 g (assumption). One competitor already lists a 530 g bag, which suggests it can be done.
  • Sized by class band, not one size for ages 5 to 15.
  • Padded, ventilated back panel and wide straps. Standard features, not a claim of orthopaedic benefit.
  • A weigh-in card in the box: hang it, weigh it, compare it.

Concept spec, none of it built or lab-tested. The image is an illustration, not a real product.

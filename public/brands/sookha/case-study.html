Case study: a rain jacket for the scooter commute

Concept project. Researched October 2026. No product exists. Name is provisional.

The short version

Rider rain jackets already exist at about Rs 1,800-2,000. The open question is whether people in work clothes want a zip-free, thigh-length one badly enough to pay Rs 2,299.

Established from sources
  • Rider-specific rain jackets from Rynox, Solace, Axor and Varsha are on sale now.
  • Some owners in one Reddit thread report zip leaks. That is anecdote, not a rate.
Still a hypothesis
  • A flap placket seals as well as a zip in sideways rain.
  • Commuters in work clothes want this cut, and a women's cut matters to them.
  • Rs 2,299 is a price people accept.

What would prove this wrong: A shower-rig test where the flap leaks as much as a good zip, or 20 commuters who say their current jacket is fine.

The brief

Find a real gap in an Indian physical-product category and build a brand around it, end to end, without made-up numbers.

What the research found

  • Two-wheelers are the daily vehicle for a very large number of Indians: 19.6 million units sold domestically in FY25 (SIAM Annual Report 2025). That is sales, not riders, and I found no reliable rainwear market size, so none is used here.
  • Cheap plastic raincoats (about Rs 100) are the everyday choice in some regions (Dailyhunt, coastal Karnataka).
  • In one Reddit thread, owners report leaks at zips on Decathlon and Zeel jackets, and a Wildcraft jacket that lets water seep in. Duckback and Zeel are also praised for lasting years. Anecdotal.

Correction to my first pass

I first thought nobody served riders. Wrong. Rynox (H2GO Pro 4, Rs 1,990), Solace (Rainpro, Rs 1,799-1,850), Axor and Varsha already sell rider rain jackets and suits. The gap is not "rain gear for riders". It is smaller.

The gap, as a hypothesis

Existing rider gear is built to go over a riding jacket and looks like riding gear. A person in office clothes on a scooter is not the customer it is designed for. A zip-free, thigh-length, work-clothes cut, with a women's pattern and a bag cover, may be underserved. This needs checking with real commuters before any money is spent.

Strategy

  • Positioning: "Dry at the office." One promise: it does not leak.
  • Product: one jacket, two cuts, two colours (teal, ochre), a bag cover and a pouch.
  • Price: Rs 2,299 target, an assumption. Above plastic and basic sets, near rider jackets.
  • Proof: a filmed shower-rig test, then a month with 20 commuters. Publish failures.
  • Channel: direct online, launch in May ahead of the monsoon, short-run first batch, Pune/Mumbai/Bengaluru first.
  • Measure: leak failures, returns, and whether buyers are office commuters or riders.

What I do not know

  • Whether enough commuters want a work-clothes cut at Rs 2,299.
  • Whether a flap placket seals as well as a good zip in sideways rain. That is the whole bet.
  • Unit cost, minimum order sizes and seam-taping capacity. Not researched.
  • The name Sookha was searched on the web only. Not a trademark check.

Move the light, not the object. A small study in how direction, softness and colour change a scene.

LIVE LIGHT STUDY / 01

TAKE THE LIGHTING DESK

An illustrative browser experiment. CSS gradients approximate a light study; this is not a physically accurate renderer or a live generation tool.

Direction

Shift the highlight across the sphere. The shadow moves to the opposite side, changing which part of the shape catches your eye.

Softness

A sharp shadow feels graphic. A diffuse shadow feels quieter. The object stays the same; the visual emphasis changes.

Colour

Try the same setup in cool blue and warm amber. Decide which mood fits the story before building the rest of the frame.

Concept, design and interaction by Anurag Dutta. Built as a hands-on creative study.
